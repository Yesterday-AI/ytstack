## What this does

- Frontier: after the slices, one line `lesson-F###<TAB><state><TAB><detail>` per lesson of `kind: lesson` that is `open` or `planned` with a `check:` target. Its state comes from the lanes table (merged: done with a reminder to set the lesson fixed; held: claimed; blocked) and from questions that name it; a lesson without `## Files` or `## Verification` is blocked as "unplanned". Without a milestone the lesson lanes are still listed; with neither, `list` fails as before.
- Brief: `render --lesson F###` takes the lesson file as the plan: the outcome from its title, target and "Assessment", scope and verification from its sections, ref and commit prefix `lesson-F###`; review briefs work with `--kind review --head`.
