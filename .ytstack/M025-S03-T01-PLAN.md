## What this does

`floor` and `route` print `lenses:`, the lenses of `REVIEW.md` (read at the base) whose `applies` globs match a changed path.

`route` gets its fourth condition. For every required lens the plan carries a line `- Lens <name>: <head> -- pass|fail|not run; by: <reviewer>; evidence: <dir> (<n> images)|none` under `## Review`. The newest line of a lens counts. It has to say `pass`, name a reviewer other than `--author` and be valid for the head: it names the head, or no path under the lens's `applies` changed since. A `reads` document that does not exist at the head means the lens cannot run. Each failure is one `reason:` line with the subject `lens <name>`.

For a lens with `evidence: visual` the directory the line names exists and holds a `MANIFEST` with at least one image line. Every listed image is a file in that directory with the listed SHA-256, and its line states `url`, `viewport` and `shows`. The line format is `<sha256>  <file name> | url: <url> | viewport: <w>x<h> | shows: <text>`.

`bin/ytstack-risk manifest <dir>` writes the first two columns for every image in a directory and keeps what a line already said, so a reviewer does not hash by hand or pick between `shasum` and `sha256sum`.
