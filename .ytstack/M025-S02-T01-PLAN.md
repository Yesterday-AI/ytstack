## What this does

An area file may carry `auto_merge: none | low | normal`, the highest risk class that may merge into the area without a person (`docs/ux/review-policy-format.md`, "Ceilings"). `bin/ytstack-spec lint` fails on any other value. A file without the field stays valid.

`.ytstack/spec/REVIEW.md` is the project's review policy. It lives beside the area files and is not one of them: `lint` does not ask it for area frontmatter or the fixed sections, and `drift`, `impact` and `areas` do not read it. When it exists, `lint` fails unless `SPEC.md` links it.

The call from `lint` into `bin/ytstack-risk check` lands with M025-S02-T02, where that command exists.

## Spec impact

- checks-and-evals: Behavior -- "The spec tool": `lint` fails on an `auto_merge` outside the three values and on a `REVIEW.md` that `SPEC.md` does not link; `REVIEW.md` is not an area file
- artifacts: Behavior -- "Files and writers": the row for `.ytstack/spec/REVIEW.md` and the optional `auto_merge` field of an area file
