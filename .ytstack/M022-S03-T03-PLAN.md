## What this does

Step 8a runs only when the closed slice was the last one of the milestone and the project has `.ytstack/spec/`. The areas to look at are the ones named under `## Spec` in the milestone's summaries, plus the ones the drift report listed in Step 3a. Per area the skill reads the area file against the milestone's outcome and brings over what applies from now on: a changed behaviour or contract, a decision in force that exists only in the milestone context (as a `DECISIONS.md` entry linked under "Decisions that apply now"), a shortcut or limit that still holds, and a raised `status` with `verified_at` only for what was read in the code.

Narrative, dates and task refs stay in the summaries. The report says per area which sections changed, or "nothing to fold". After the fold the skill names the archive offer of `ytstack:reconcile` (its "closed milestones" class) and moves nothing itself. Without a technical spec the step is skipped and the report points at `ytstack:adopt-brownfield`. Checklist item 9 names the fold before the backlog sweep.
