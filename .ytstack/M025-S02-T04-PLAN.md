## What this does

Preference half: `yt_prefs` sets `YT_PREF_MERGE_BY` from `merge_by:` in `PREFERENCES.md`. The values are `human`, `coordinator` and `risk`; the default is `risk`. A value outside the three is reported in `YT_PREF_WARNINGS` and read as `human`, not as the default: a typo never lets a change merge without a person. `yt_prefs` also sets `YT_PREF_EVIDENCE_DIR` from `evidence_dir:`, default `.ytstack/evidence`, without a trailing slash.

Skill half: `finish-lane` reads `merge_by` with the new default and the third value. With `risk` it runs `bin/ytstack-risk route` and merges on its own only when the route is `unattended` and the host's gates hold. The preamble of `skills/finish-lane/SKILL.md` reads the preference with its own default (`human`) and its own case statement today; that is the place to change.

`bin/ytstack-finish-lane route --lane <ref> --base <sha> --head <sha>` is the one call the skill makes for it. It runs in the checkout of the coordinating session. The author of the change is the worker in the lane's row of `LANES.md` (the first word of "Claimed by"), the plan is `<ref>-PLAN.md`. It prints one line of what it read and then the lines of `bin/ytstack-risk route`, and exits with that script's code. A lane without a row has no known author and a lane without a plan has no review record: both are `person`. `gates` and `merge` stay as they are: they read the host and nothing else.

The skill gets a step "Read the route" between the recorded review and the gates, and a table in "Merge or hand over": `risk` with `unattended` merges and tells the person in one line; `risk` with `person` stops with the command and the reasons; `human` always stops; `coordinator` merges and prints the route. A standing instruction that reserves the merge wins. On a host other than GitHub the skill does the steps that need only git, the review at the local head and the route included, and stops. `coordinate` and `spawn-milestone-team` name the route where they speak about merging.

The eval case `risk-route` holds a lane whose change breaks an invariant in code only while the floor says `low`: the round line has to carry `risk: high`, and nothing is merged.
