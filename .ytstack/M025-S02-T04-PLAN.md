## What this does

Preference half: `yt_prefs` sets `YT_PREF_MERGE_BY` from `merge_by:` in `PREFERENCES.md`. The values are `human`, `coordinator` and `risk`; the default is `risk`. A value outside the three is reported in `YT_PREF_WARNINGS` and read as `human`, not as the default: a typo never lets a change merge without a person. `yt_prefs` also sets `YT_PREF_EVIDENCE_DIR` from `evidence_dir:`, default `.ytstack/evidence`, without a trailing slash.

Skill half: `finish-lane` reads `merge_by` with the new default and the third value. With `risk` it runs `bin/ytstack-risk route` and merges on its own only when the route is `unattended` and the host's gates hold. The preamble of `skills/finish-lane/SKILL.md` reads the preference with its own default (`human`) and its own case statement today; that is the place to change.
