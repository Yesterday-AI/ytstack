## What this does

An incident gets one record, `.ytstack/incidents/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<slug>.md`. `docs/ux/signal-format.md` gains its format: the frontmatter `id`, `state` (`open`, `mitigated`, `closed`), `opened`, `mitigated`, `closed`, `severity` and `root_cause`, and six sections in a fixed order: "What is seen", "Timeline", "Hypotheses", "Proposed mitigations", "Decisions" and "After". A mitigation is one list line with what it changes, the expected effect, the risk, the undo, the path of its handover script and its status.

`bin/ytstack-signals incident new --title <text> [--severity <text>]` writes the skeleton with the time it was opened and prints the path; it never overwrites a record. `incident list [--state <state>]` prints the records that are not closed, so that a session can find the one to resume.

`check` validates the records as well: the name and the id, the state and its times, a root cause on a closed record, the six sections in order, and every mitigation line. A mitigation that is not `declined` must name a script that exists and passes `bin/ytstack-handover check`; one that was carried out must name a receipt that exists. The last line becomes `signals ok: <n> finding(s), <m> incident(s)`.
