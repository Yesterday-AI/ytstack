## What this does

`bin/ytstack-risk route --plan <plan file> --base <sha> --head <sha> [--author <worker>]` computes the floor, reads the newest round line under `## Review` of the plan and prints `route: unattended` or `route: person`, then `class:`, `ceiling:`, `floor:`, `areas:` and one `reason:` line per condition that failed. Exit 0 for `unattended`, 10 for `person`, 64 for a usage error, 65 when git cannot answer.

The route is `unattended` only when the floor is not `person`, the newest round line approves, states `risk` and `invariants: none`, comes from a reviewer other than `--author` and is valid for the head, and the class (the higher of floor and stated risk) is not above the ceiling.

The round line is read tolerantly: a line without `risk`, `invariants` and `by` is a round that does not state them, with one reason that names what is missing. Without `--author` the route is `person`, because the script cannot tell the reviewer from the author.

A round is valid for a head when it names that head, or when nothing but process records changed between its head and that head. The round line itself is written into a plan after the review, so a rule without that exemption would never hold for a committed plan.
