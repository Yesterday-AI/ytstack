## What this does

`skills/minimal-code/SKILL.md` is the one text that says how much work is enough: the smallest complete change with the smallest sufficient evidence. It keeps its name, the `minimal_code` preference and the six-rung ladder. Before the ladder stands "Reach before size": list every place the change must reach, then pick the smallest solution that covers all of it. A bug fix greps every caller and fixes the cause once in the shared code. A trivial change (a rename, a constant, a text, a config value, a moved line) gets no new test, and a `shortcut:` comment names its limit and when to upgrade.

The section "Evidence" bounds checking the way the ladder bounds code: run what the plan's Verification names, as often as it says, in the environment it names. Evidence is a check on a commit in an environment and does not expire because a new session reads it. A finding the project's policy rates non-blocking is a follow-up, and a verdict only a person can give is never recorded as given. Every report ends with "Not checked:". The file stays under 60 lines.

The SessionStart line names both halves of the contract and the closing line. The stop note in `using-ytstack` covers every worker dispatched with a brief, on any host, and says that the contract in the brief applies. The TDD wrapper says which changes get a failing test first and which get none. `NOTICE` credits the concepts taken from ponytail 5.3.3.
