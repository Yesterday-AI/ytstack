## What this does

```
yt_find_artifacts <yt_dir> <glob>     # prints matching paths, one per line, newest first (ls -t), from:
   <yt_dir>/<glob>  <yt_dir>/milestones/*/<glob>  <yt_dir>/milestones/<glob>  <yt_dir>/decisions/<glob>  <yt_dir>/backlog/<glob>  <yt_dir>/backlog/*/<glob>  <yt_dir>/quick/<glob>
```
Callers replace their `ls "$YT_DIR"/*-SUMMARY.md` style lookups with `yt_find_artifacts "$YT_DIR" '*-SUMMARY.md'`; summarize-task's plan lookup tries `yt_find_artifacts "$_YT_DIR" "$_TASK_REF-PLAN.md" | head -1` when the flat path is missing. Tests: flat fixture unchanged; nested fixture (`milestones/M001/M001-S01-T01-SUMMARY.md`) found by session-start's "Recent tasks" and by `yt_find_artifacts`.
