## What this does

Issue #54: a coordinator hand-built a PR babysitter, a review tiering and a release check that the plugin already ships, and a coordinator that merged itself never passed `finish-lane`, so no route applied. Step 7, which held the PR watch and `ytstack-watch`, becomes the one place that names every factory tool:

| Tool | Command | When | Skipped when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Route | `ytstack-finish-lane route --lane`, or `ytstack-risk route --plan --author` for a pull request without a lane | before every merge, also one this session makes itself | never before a merge |
| PR watch | `ytstack-pr-watch register --repo <owner/name> --all-open`, no `--session` | start, restart, after a worker opened a PR | flag off or no heartbeat under 10 minutes: `ytstack-watch --pr ... --interval 60` |
| Release watch | `ytstack-watch release --commit <merge commit>` | after every merge that goes to production | `finish-lane` watched it; no "Release" section |
| Signals | `ytstack:signal-intake`, `ytstack:incident` | slice boundary, after a release receipt, a person's word; incident on an outage | no "Signals" sources; incident never on a guess |
| Review lessons | `ytstack:review-lessons` | after a merge with a bot finding | no own reviewers; `finish-lane` ran it |

The PR watch paragraph of Step 7 is kept once, under the table. A merge this session makes outside `finish-lane` follows `finish-lane` Step 11 for who merges and merges through `ytstack-finish-lane merge`. The preamble prints `MERGE_BY`, `REVIEW_POLICY`, `OWN_REVIEWERS`, `PR_WATCH` (flag and heartbeat age), `RELEASE` and `SIGNALS`, so every skip condition is read, not guessed. The Terminal State allows `signal-intake`, `incident` and `review-lessons`.

The test: the preamble lines on the bare fixture and with `REVIEW.md` own reviewers, a "Release" and a "Signals" section; and every `ytstack-<tool> <subcommand>` that Step 7 names exists in `bin/` and its usage header names the subcommand.
