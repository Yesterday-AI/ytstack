## What this does

`visual-review` is a native procedure skill for the reviewer of a lens with `evidence: visual` (`docs/ux/review-policy-format.md`, "Lenses"), and for a person who asks for a visual review of a commit. The reviewer is not the author of the change.

The preamble prints where `bin/ytstack-runtime` and `bin/ytstack-risk` are, whether `RUNTIME.md` has a "Run" section, the evidence directory and the root it starts at (the main checkout of the repository), the commit, and the visual lenses of `REVIEW.md`.

The procedure: establish commit, lens, lane and the documents of the lens; stop with the verdict `not run` when there is no "Run" section, no browser tool, or the product does not come up; add a worktree of its own at the reviewed commit and start the product there with `bin/ytstack-runtime up`; list the views, states, widths and themes the change affects; operate the product in the browser, take its own screenshots and look at each one against the documents; store the images with a `MANIFEST` (`bin/ytstack-risk manifest`) in the evidence directory; stop the product and remove the worktree; write the lens line and the report. It never edits a product file and fixes nothing.

A dispatched reviewer returns the lens line in its report. A session a person asked directly appends it to the plan of the lane under `## Review`.

The eval case `visual-review` has a visual lens and a "Run" section whose start command cannot work: the lens line has to say `not run`, never `pass`.
