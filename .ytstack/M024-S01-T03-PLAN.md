## What this does

`bin/ytstack-brief render --kind implement|review|prepare` adds a `## Kind` section that says what the worker may do. `implement` is the default and renders the same brief as no `--kind`. `review` is read only: the brief pins one commit with `--head`, or the range from `--base` to `--head`, and has no Git section. `prepare` writes files, commits nothing and lists them under `Uncommitted:`. The sentences per kind live in `brief-template.md`.

The report has eleven labels: `Status:`, `Uncommitted:` and `Next:` joined the eight of M023. `check` accepts `complete`, `partial` and `blocked` as `Status:` and nothing else. `Status: blocked` needs a `Blockers:` value other than `none`, and `Status: complete` needs `Blockers: none`.

`codex/adapter.md` and `docs/codex.md` tell a Codex worker and its orchestrator about the kind. `minimal-code` says that every report names what was not checked. `resume-session` reads the "Suggested skills" section of a handoff.
