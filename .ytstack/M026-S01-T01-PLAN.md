## What this does

`ytstack-watch release --commit <sha> [--timeout <seconds>] [--interval <seconds>] [-C <dir>]` reads `deployed`, `healthy`, `settle` and `target` of the "Release" section through `bin/ytstack-runtime get`. Without the section or without `deployed` it prints one line that names `docs/ux/runtime-format.md` and exits with a code of its own; nothing is run.

It runs `deployed` every interval until the output contains the commit. The commit matches as a full SHA or as a prefix of at least seven characters, in either direction: the target may print a short SHA while the caller passes the full one. When the timeout ends (default 900 seconds) the receipt says that the commit never arrived and what the target printed last.

Then it runs `healthy`, when there is one, every interval for the settle time (default 120 seconds) and counts how often it was asked and how often it failed. One failure makes the result `unhealthy`.

While it waits it prints a line only when something changes. At the end it prints one receipt in fixed labelled lines, each host fact with the UTC time it was read: target, commit, arrival, health, result (`released`, `not arrived`, `unhealthy`, `no health command`). It runs only the two commands from `RUNTIME.md`: it deploys nothing and rolls nothing back, and the usage text says so.

Exit codes are distinct for released, not arrived, unhealthy, no "Release" section and usage. They are documented at the top of the script and in the "Release" section of `docs/ux/runtime-format.md`.
