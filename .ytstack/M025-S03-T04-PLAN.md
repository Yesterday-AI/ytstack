## What this does

A change whose paths match the `applies` of a lens needs that lens before it can take the route without a person (`docs/ux/review-policy-format.md`, "Lenses"). `bin/ytstack-risk floor` names the lenses under `lenses:`.

`code-review` (the session's own task) and `finish-lane` (a lane) read that line once the round is approved. For every lens they render the lens brief (`bin/ytstack-brief render --kind review --lens <name>`), dispatch one separate fresh reviewer with it, check its report and record one lens line under `## Review` of the plan. `fail` is handled like blocking findings. `not run` is recorded as it is. A lens line of an earlier head is not renewed while `route` still accepts it. `finish-lane` gets the step "Dispatch the required lenses" between the recorded round and the route. `coordinate` and `spawn-milestone-team` say that a lens reviewer is a lane of kind `review`.

`bin/ytstack-finish-lane`: files under the evidence directory (`evidence_dir`) are a record of the review and no leftover of the lane; `leftovers` and `commit` leave them out. The suite pins that, and that `route` reads the evidence of a visual lens from the main checkout when the session works in a linked worktree.
