- Create: `skills/lane-retro/SKILL.md`

## What this does

`lane-retro` looks at a few finished lanes while coordinated work is still running. Its preamble lists the finished lanes of `LANES.md` (merged ones, and blocked ones as the lanes that went wrong) and the lanes an earlier lane retro sampled. The sample is about one finished lane in five, the ones that went wrong first.

Per lane the skill reads four things and nothing else: the brief, the report, the review findings and the row. It examines them in six fixed categories: navigation pointers, automated checks, review standards, instruction lines that change nothing, tool economy and access to information. A category without evidence gets "nothing found".

A candidate needs evidence (the lane, the file and the line) and proposes exactly one of two things: a check that replaces a named instruction line, or a pointer that replaces a search. The candidates go into `.ytstack/retro/LANES-<date>.md` and to the human in one question. An accepted candidate becomes a task line or a quick task. The skill implements nothing itself, and it never answers a mistake with a new rule in prose.
