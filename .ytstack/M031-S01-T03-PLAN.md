## What this does

Issue #61: a coordinator ran through two compactions because open lanes and an incident never left a quiet moment for the rotation. The rotation becomes one step that does not wait:

1. Before writing: the rows' next actions, `ytstack-frontier check`, `ytstack-questions check`, the standing instructions complete (as today).
2. `HANDOFF.md` written directly in the format of `ytstack:handoff-session`, without its four questions (the answers are in the files), with a "Rotation" block in "In-flight work": the PR watch session of this session, the background watches with their commands, one line per running worker (lane, name, address, worktree, branch, dispatch time).
3. The user is told one line: start a fresh session with `ytstack:coordinate` and leave this one open until the listed workers reported.
4. From then on the old session is a relay: per arriving report `ytstack-brief check`, `ytstack-frontier set <lane> --status reported --next ...`, the report appended under "Reports after the rotation" in `HANDOFF.md`. Nothing else.

The successor's Step 3: a handoff with a "Rotation" block makes it run `ytstack-pr-watch handover --from <session>` (T01) before the registration of Step 7, start the listed watches again, and leave the rows of the listed workers alone until their reports arrive in the handoff (or the person says the old session closed; then the existing rule for a lost worker applies).
