## What this does

`finish-lane` Step 7 writes the round line first and puts it last into the body of the review it posts on `{SHA}`; the plan gets the same line only where the session may write, and never through a commit on the lane's branch. Step 8 posts the lens lines as one more review, whose first line is the verdict the gate of Step 10 matches (`Verdict: approved` unless a lens failed). Step 9 runs `route --pr {N}` and reports the `round:` source. `code-review` posts its round and lens lines on the branch's open pull request when its head is the reviewed commit, and says why no commit should carry the line. `code-review` gains `Write` for the body file.

The spec areas name `--pr` in the paragraphs on both scripts (checks-and-evals, `verified_at` to the commit of T02) and the posted records in "The class of a change" (skills). `docs/concept.md` names the host record beside the `## Review` lines.
