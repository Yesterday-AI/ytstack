## What this does

`bin/ytstack-frontier list` prints one line per slice of the milestone: `done`, `claimed`, `blocked` or `ready`, with a detail. It reads the checkboxes of the milestone roadmap, the `**Blocked by:**` line of each slice plan and `.ytstack/LANES.md`. `--ready` prints the ready lanes only and exits 1 when there is none.

`claim <lane> --by <worker>` adds the lane's row with status `claimed` and creates the file when it is missing. A held lane is refused with exit 1 and the holder's name and time, and nothing is written. `set` changes named cells of one row, `release` frees it, `check` validates the ten columns, unique lanes, the listed Status and Kind values and a claim time on every held lane.

`slice-milestone` writes `**Blocked by:**` under the goal of every slice plan and names only real dependencies. `plan-task` writes the line in a task plan only when the task needs another task's result.

`spawn-milestone-team` asks the frontier what may start, claims each ready lane before anything goes to a worker, records each stage with `set`, and looks at the frontier again whenever a lane is verified or merged. A changed `.ytstack/LANES.md` alone does not make the tree dirty for its gate.
