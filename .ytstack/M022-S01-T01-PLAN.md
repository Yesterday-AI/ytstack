## What this does

`docs/ux/spec-format.md` is the contract for the technical spec. It defines `SPEC.md` (one page, at most 80 lines, with the "Keeping this true" block) and the area file: frontmatter `area`, `status`, `paths`, `sources`, `verified_at`, and eleven section names in a fixed order, four of them required. It states the pointer-only rule for an area that already has an authoritative document. It holds the maintenance rules for a plan, for closure, for the drift report, for decisions and for scaffolds, and it lists what `bin/ytstack-spec lint` fails and warns on.

The glossary separates "technical spec" from the design-doc "spec". The DECISIONS entry records why the spec gets artifacts of its own and how the rules apply. `ROADMAP.md` gets the plan section for issues #35, #36 and #34 with the four corrections of the review comment, and the M022 row. The five milestone files hold the goal, the exit criteria, eleven locked decisions and the three slices.
