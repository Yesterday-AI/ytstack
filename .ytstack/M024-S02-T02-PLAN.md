## What this does

`finish-lane` is the procedure for the session that dispatched a lane, around the calls of `bin/ytstack-finish-lane`. The order is fixed: read the report and commit leftovers, push and open the pull request, wait until the host shows the head, check the body, commission the review, post it and record head and round, read the gates, merge or hand over.

The pre-review checklist has a mechanical half (`body-check`) and a reading half (the body once against the diff and its tests). The review is a `review` lane in a fresh context, pinned to the commit `wait-head` printed: round 1 the whole change, round 2 the fix, no third round but a diagnosis for the human. A finding about the body alone gets a delta brief on the same head and no new round.

The reviewed commit and the round go into the lane's row with `bin/ytstack-frontier set`. A failing gate is handled by what it is. Who merges is `merge_by:` in `PREFERENCES.md`: with `human`, the default, the skill stops and hands over the merge command; with `coordinator` it calls `merge`. On a host other than GitHub the skill stops after the commit step.
