## What this does

`plan-task` Step 6 asks for the command and for how often and where it runs. One pass in the local checkout is the default. More than one pass, a sample and another environment carry their reason in the plan. The plan template writes `Runs: <n>. Environment: <where>.` under the command, with `Sample:` when the check covers a sample. That section is all the evidence the task owes: execution adds no passes, scenarios, suites or environments and drops none.

What work in the task cannot produce is not written as a verification command: a person's verdict, an approval, a measurement on a system the task does not control. It becomes a line under `## Gates`: `- [ ] G01 -- <what> | decides: <who> | how: <how> | lapses: <when>`. A field nobody stated is `unknown`. A gate does not hold the task open; the task closes on its verification.

`slice-milestone` writes slice gates in the same format. "Done when" holds only what work in the slice can establish. A slice is closed by work when its tasks are done and accepted when its gates are given or lapsed. An expensive verification is not a gate: it belongs in the task plan's Verification section with its reason.

## Spec impact

- artifacts: Behavior -- "States and closure": a task plan's Verification section holds the command and a line `Runs: <n>. Environment: <where>.`, with `Sample:` and a reason; `## Gates` in a slice plan or task plan holds one line per gate
- skills: Behavior -- "The work contract", Plan: the Verification section names the command, `Runs:` and `Environment:`; what work cannot produce is a gate line, and a gate never holds a task open
