## What this does

The preamble prints three things for the current milestone. Between `OPEN_GATES_BEGIN` and `OPEN_GATES_END`: every open gate line of its plans, with file and line number. Between `REVIEW_ROUNDS_BEGIN` and `REVIEW_ROUNDS_END`: every task of the slice whose plan records three or more review rounds. And the numbers `SLICES_TOTAL`, `SLICES_CLOSED_BY_WORK` (every task checked) and `SLICES_ACCEPTED` (closed by work, and no gate open in the slice plan or its task plans).

Step 3b reports the two numbers side by side and sorts each open gate into one group: needs a verdict, lapsed, or work can still close it. The first two, and work that repeats without advancing, go to the human in ONE question, each with who decides, how, and when it lapses. A gate that work can still close is not listed.

The skill records only what the human answered, in the plan that holds the gate: `- [x] ... | given: <date> <who>` or `- [-] ... | lapsed: <date> <what happened>`. A gate that was not given stays open. A lapsed gate is never recorded as passed. In non-interactive mode the skill asks nothing and records nothing; it lists the items under "Waiting on a person". The status line and the closing report carry both numbers.
