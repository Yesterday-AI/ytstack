## What this does

- `coordinate`: `LESSONS: <n> open (<p> proposals, <c> checks to build)` and the open lessons between `LESSONS_BEGIN` and `LESSONS_END`; every status names the count and the classes that repeat; the oldest open lesson proposal is the question asked once a UTC day (after a question that blocks a lane); a lesson lane is claimed with `set --state planned --ref lesson-F###`, rendered with `--lesson`, and its lesson set `fixed` after the merge.
- `retro`: with `LESSONS` above 0, step 8 runs `ytstack-signals lesson list --state all --since <window start>` and shows per area and class the incidents in the window, all time, whether it repeated (count 2 or more, or a reopened lesson), where the lesson lives and its state; repeated classes whose lesson is a proposal or a knowledge entry, and reopened ones, are candidates for "3 Things to Improve"; the snapshot gets `lessons`.
