## What this does

A review states the class of the change it looked at, and the plan keeps that statement where `bin/ytstack-risk route` reads it (`docs/ux/review-policy-format.md`, "Records").

`code-review`: the preamble prints what `bin/ytstack-risk floor` computed for the task's commit range. The reviewer gets those lines and ends its report with `Risk:`, `Severity:`, `Reach:` and `Invariants touched:`. It reads the change against "Invariants and security boundaries" and "Decisions that apply now" of the area files it already receives, and names a rule the change shifts also when only code changed. The round line gains `risk`, `invariants` and `by`. `by` is the dispatched reviewer; a session that reviews its own change without one writes `by: self`. The round counting of the preamble reads old and new lines.

The review brief (`bin/ytstack-brief render --kind review`) asks the reviewer of a lane for the same four labels: the sentences live in `brief-template.md`. `bin/ytstack-brief check` validates the four labels when a report carries `Risk:`.

`finish-lane`: the review step sends a report without the four labels back once, and appends the extended round line to the lane's plan under `## Review`, in the checkout of the coordinating session.
