## What this does

`docs/ux/coordinator-format.md` fixes the formats of coordinator mode in one document, before any script or skill uses them. It opens with two rules for everything a coordinator writes: entries are written as history with their UTC time, and host facts are never stored as current.

It then defines `LANES.md` (ten columns, eight status values, one writer besides a human), the `**Blocked by:**` line of a slice plan and the frontier that follows from it, the three lane kinds (`implement`, `review`, `prepare`), the report with its eleven labels and the three `Status:` values, one file per question under `questions/` with the states open, asked and answered, and `STANDING.md` for instructions that hold beyond the task. The last section names what each script checks mechanically.

The `DECISIONS.md` entry locks in the choice and its rules of use: the lanes table is not part of `STATE.md`, a status names when a host fact was read, `merge_by:` decides who merges, and GitHub through `gh` is the only host. The milestone context, the roadmap and the four slice plans carry the plan of M024.
