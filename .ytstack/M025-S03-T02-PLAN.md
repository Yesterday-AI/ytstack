## What this does

A lens is a second, independent review with one question (`docs/ux/review-policy-format.md`, "Lenses"). Its reviewer gets a brief of its own.

`render --task|--slice <ref> --kind review --lens <name> --head <sha>` adds a section "Lens" to the review brief: the lens line of `.ytstack/spec/REVIEW.md`, the list of its `reads` documents, the rules that hold for every lens, and for `evidence: visual` the evidence rules, the instruction to load `ytstack:visual-review`, and where the images go: `<evidence_dir>/<ref>/<short sha>/<lens>`, read from the root of the main checkout of the repository. A lens that names a `skill` gets the sentence to load it. The "Kind" section says that this is a lens review with the verdict `pass`, `fail` or `not run`; it does not ask for the class of the change.

The lens is read from the `REVIEW.md` of the reviewed commit. The command refuses, without printing a brief, a lens that this file does not define and a lens whose `reads` document does not exist at that commit.

The lens report has the eleven labels plus `Lens: <name> -- <pass | fail | not run>` and `Evidence: <directory> (<n> images)` or `Evidence: none`. `bin/ytstack-brief check` validates the two when a report carries one of them.
